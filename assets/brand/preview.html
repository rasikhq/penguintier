Penguintier icons

The final icon set (concept A, "Orbit", picked on 2026-10-19), drawn from the real files in assets/: the app icon at every size, the tray states in color and as monochrome "symbolic" icons, inside a mock GNOME top bar and desktop panels. The three original concepts are further down.

Final

Orbit

App icon: icons/hicolor/scalable/apps/io.github.rasikhq.Penguintier.svg (32 px and up) and brand/app-icon-small.svg (16–24 px). Tray: tray/penguintier-{online,offline,attention}[-symbolic].svg. Run python3 assets/render-icons.py after editing an SVG.

Concepts

The three concepts first shown. A was picked.

A · Orbit a node in a network
B · Peers linked to two peers
C · Beacon online and reachable
A

Orbit

The penguin stands inside a tilted orbit, with one peer riding the ring: a node in a network, or a tiny planet. The ring passes behind its head and in front of its belly. The strongest silhouette of the three.

B

Peers

The penguin links out to two peers, like the familiar network/share glyph. The most literal "network" reading; calm and technical.

C

Beacon

The penguin between two signal arcs: "I'm online and reachable". Symmetric, and the easiest to read at 16 px.
